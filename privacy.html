Privacy Policy

Effective — 2026

This policy explains what information The Camel Family Office ("we," "us," "our") collects through thecamelfamilyoffice.com, how it is used, and how it is protected. We apply the same standard of discretion described in our Confidentiality commitments to everything covered here.

Information We Collect

The only information this website collects is what you choose to submit through the Secure Intake Protocol form:

  • Legal entity identity (required)
  • Institutional affiliation (optional)
  • A digital identifier or public key, if you choose to provide one (optional)
  • Your strategic alignment objective, in your own words (required)

Providing this information is voluntary. The two fields marked required are needed for us to evaluate and respond to your enquiry; without them, we cannot process your submission. All other fields may be left blank with no consequence.

We do not use cookies, analytics scripts, or any form of visitor tracking on this website. No information is collected simply by browsing the site.

How Your Submission Is Transmitted

When you submit the intake form, your browser opens a pre-filled email addressed to CamelFamilyOffice@proton.me using your own email application. Nothing is sent to us, and nothing is stored on any server, until you personally choose to send that email. This website has no backend or database — it is a static page, and the form cannot transmit data on its own.

How We Use Your Information

Information you send us is used solely to evaluate and respond to your enquiry. We do not sell, rent, or trade it. As stated in our Confidentiality commitments, the identity of anyone who contacts us — and the fact that they contacted us at all — is treated as confidential, and is never disclosed to a third party without your explicit written consent.

Third Parties & Cross-Border Transfer

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure is based in the United States; it serves the static files you see but does not receive or process the intake form's contents. If you choose to send us the pre-filled email, it is handled by ProtonMail, our email provider, whose infrastructure is based in Switzerland, under its own zero-access encryption policies. Submitting the form therefore involves a transfer of your information outside Lebanon, to these two jurisdictions only. We do not share your information with any other third party, and we do not use it for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Retention

Because submissions arrive as ordinary email rather than through a database, retention is governed by our own email records management, consistent with the confidentiality obligations described on this site. You may request that we delete correspondence you've sent us at any time by contacting us at the address above.

Your Rights

You may ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request its correction, or request its deletion. Contact us at CamelFamilyOffice@proton.me to make any such request.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for institutional and professional use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

Lebanese Data Protection Notice

The Camel Family Office is registered in Lebanon. This notice is intended to satisfy the disclosure obligations of Lebanon's Law No. 81/2018 on Electronic Transactions and Personal Data, which requires, among other things, that the identity of the data collector, the purpose of collection, whether providing data is mandatory or voluntary, the recipients of the data, and the retention period be clearly disclosed to anyone whose personal data is collected. Those disclosures appear in the sections above.

Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws applicable in the jurisdictions in which The Camel Family Office operates, including Lebanon and Qatar, and is subject to the internal protocols of the Al Jamal Family Constitution.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to CamelFamilyOffice@proton.me.
